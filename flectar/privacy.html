Privacy Policy

This OAuth app registration is used only by its owner, for personal use, to let the Flectar Mail desktop client access the owner's own Gmail account.

  • Email data is accessed only by the mail client running on the owner's own device.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with anyone by this registration.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
